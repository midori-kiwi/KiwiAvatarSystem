3.2 Pose16 snapshot, immutability, and lifetime

- Pose16 is a readonly struct containing sixteen readonly float values copied
  out of the transient package protobuf inside the native callback.
- AcceptedSnapshot is also readonly and contains the Pose16 plus immutable
  graph/sample/generation/backend/dimension identity.
- TryGetAcceptedSnapshot copies the whole value while holding the service lock;
  no package object, Packet, MatrixData, pointer, or mutable array escapes.
- One accepted snapshot is replaced atomically on a newer valid completion.
- BeginShutdown and RequestGraphReset clear it.
- The consumer re-reads the current snapshot and compares HasSameIdentity before
  commit, preventing replacement between initial lookup and writer adoption.
