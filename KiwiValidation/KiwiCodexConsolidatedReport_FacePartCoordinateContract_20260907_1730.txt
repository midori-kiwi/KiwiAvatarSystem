5. COORDINATE_PARITY_PROOF
============================================================

Definitions:
- M=(x,y): MediaPipe normalized semantic point, x right and y down in the submitted image.
- R=M: Runner stored point.
- K=R: Canonical published semantic point.
- T_expected=(x,1-y): same physical point in the unmirrored Unity source/snapshot texture UV.
- Scene mirrorX=1, so Cropper C=(1-x,1-y).
- Transaction normalized UV parity is identity, so pinned texture sampling remains C.

General proof:
- C.x == T_expected.x only when 1-x == x, i.e. x==0.5.
- A face feature is not contractually constrained to x==0.5; eyes and mouth center move with
  pose/translation and mouth center need not be exactly central even frontally.
- Therefore current X parity is generally false by construction.
- C.y == T_expected.y for every y, so current Y parity is exact.
- Clamping/edge-preservation and temporal interpolation cannot turn the global mapping 1-x
  into x. They may change a held/display rect, but do not establish point identity.

Left eye center:
- M_L=((x_362+x_263)/2,(y_362+y_263)/2).
- Expected pinned texture UV center=(M_L.x,1-M_L.y).
- Cropper center=(1-M_L.x,1-M_L.y), then swapEyes assigns this rect to rightEyeImage.
- Assignment to another output does not change the sampled UV and cannot prove
  1-M_L.x equals the original left-eye point M_L.x.
- LEFT_EYE_COORDINATE_PARITY=FAIL_X_PASS_Y.

Right eye center:
- M_R=((x_33+x_133)/2,(y_33+y_133)/2).
- Expected pinned texture UV center=(M_R.x,1-M_R.y).
- Cropper center=(1-M_R.x,1-M_R.y), then swapEyes assigns this rect to leftEyeImage.
- Symmetry with the opposite eye is not an identity contract; 1-M_R.x cannot be substituted
  for M_R.x and does not become exact through output reassignment.
- RIGHT_EYE_COORDINATE_PARITY=FAIL_X_PASS_Y.

Mouth center:
- Initial semantic anchor M_M=((x_61+x_291)/2,(y_61+y_291)/2); safe-crop may use the
  contour-derived center but that center remains in the same raw MediaPipe image space.
- Expected pinned texture UV center=(M_M.x,1-M_M.y).
- Cropper center=(1-M_M.x,1-M_M.y).
- Mouth has no left/right output swap that could even visually approximate cancellation.
- MOUTH_COORDINATE_PARITY=FAIL_X_PASS_Y.

swapEyes decision:
- MediaPipe official topology and Cropper constants agree: 362/263 are anatomical left;
  33/133 are anatomical right.
- In the unmirrored source, anatomical left appears on viewer/image right. Scene LeftEye3D is
  at anchored X=+250; RightEye3D is at X=-250. Direct anatomical assignment therefore already
  matches current output placement.
- Scene swapEyes=1 instead assigns the left semantic rect to rightEyeImage and the right rect
  to leftEyeImage. This is not a coordinate transform and is not required by the current
  unmirrored anatomical binding.
- mirrorX=1 plus swapEyes=1 can make symmetric eyes look approximately plausible because it
  samples near the opposite eye and routes that sample to the opposite output. Approximate
  facial symmetry is not exact point parity and cannot explain or repair the mouth result.
