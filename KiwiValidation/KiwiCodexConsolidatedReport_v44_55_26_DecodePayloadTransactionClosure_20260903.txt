4. PUBLIC DESIGN PRINCIPLES
======================================================================

- An asynchronous GPU readback result is a point-in-time payload associated
  with that request. A later readback or copy of the same mutable backing
  resource is a different observation unless execution/generation identity is
  independently closed.
- Object identity and schedule identity do not imply immutable content identity.
- Worker-owned or backend-owned output storage must not be treated as permanent
  transaction storage after the owner may schedule or reuse resources.
- Semantic validation must compare the values actually consumed by the
  authority path. A proxy snapshot is not authority merely because it is close
  in time or references the same tensor object.
- Logical tensor length controls semantic comparison. Backing buffer capacity
  must not enlarge or redefine the 1405-float contract.
- Diagnostic observers should be bounded, fail closed, and must not introduce
  waits, repeated inference, new backend ownership, or Production decisions.

These principles are consistent with the inspected installed 2.4.1 source.
They are not used as substitutes for the local Runtime evidence.
